About

I think in systems.
I build from scratch.

I was the first employee at Good Humans — an AI hardware startup that went from accelerator to seed funding in under six months. With no playbook and no team, I built the company's marketing engine, operations infrastructure, and growth systems entirely from scratch. I replaced entire categories of repetitive work with AI agents, LLM pipelines, and custom internal tooling — shipped two consumer features end-to-end, ran 200+ user interviews, and grew a community of 3,500+, all while the role was still being defined around me.

My path here is anything but linear. I spent five years in medical school at Beijing University of Chinese Medicine, including two years of clinical training at one of the best hospitals in China. Then came a deliberate pivot: a B.L.S. with a focus on Management Information Systems at the University of Maine — my bridge into data, systems, and the emerging world of AI. I graduated with an M.S. in Computer Science from Northeastern University in May 2026. I ended up here because tech and medicine demand the same thing: the ability to read incomplete information, make a call, and move.
